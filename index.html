What it does on a job

Catches a bad number before the water system does

Type 4.2 on an RP's first check and TagDue says so on the spot: below the 5.0 psid minimum. It also checks that check 1 sits above the relief valve opening point, that your test kit's accuracy check is inside 12 months, and that your certification has not lapsed.

Needs no signal

Pits, basements and mechanical rooms are where the work is and where phones drop out. TagDue runs entirely on your phone or tablet. Readings, signature and the PDF report are all made on the device.

Brings the customer back next year

Every assembly carries its due date. Open the list, see what is overdue or coming up in 30 and 60 days, and send the customer a reminder from your own email or phone in two taps.

Handles the repair on the same visit

Failed first test, rubber kit, passing retest: one report shows all three, the way water systems ask for it. RP, DC, PVB, SVB and detector assemblies, Type II included.

Follows your jurisdiction's rules

The 3 psid RP buffer, check 2 as a number, the Type II bypass minimum: procedures differ, so those are settings. You are the certified tester, and you can always record your own result with the reason printed on the report.

Your records stay yours

Bring in the spreadsheet you already keep. Export customers, assemblies and every test as a spreadsheet at any time, with or without a licence. Nothing is uploaded to us.

What it does not do

  • It does not file into your water system's online portal. Where a city requires its own portal, you still enter the test there. TagDue lays the values out in order with a copy button on each one to make that quick.
  • It prints one clear report layout, carrying the fields water systems ask for. It does not reproduce each city's own form.
  • It keeps records on one device. There is no cloud sync yet. A backup file moves everything to a second phone or tablet, and you should save one regularly.

TagDue flags readings against published field-test criteria. It does not replace the tester's judgment, certification or the water system's rules.
