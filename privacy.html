Privacy

TagDue keeps your records on your own device. We do not receive them.

What the app stores, and where

Your customers, assemblies, test reports, signatures, settings and licence key are saved in your browser's storage on the phone, tablet or computer you use. The app does not upload them, and it has no accounts. After it has loaded, the app makes no requests to any server other than to fetch its own files.

That also means we cannot recover your records. Use the backup feature and keep the backup file somewhere safe.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking scripts.
  • No cookies set by TagDue.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because we do not hold any.

What others may see

  • Hosting. This site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when the pages are fetched. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Reminders and reports you send. When you send a reminder or share a report, it goes through your own email, messaging or sharing app, under that app's terms.
  • Buying a licence. Payment is handled by a payment provider, which collects what it needs to take payment. We receive your name, business name and email so we can issue your licence key.

Your customers' details

You decide what you record about your customers, and you are responsible for it. Erasing a customer, or everything, is under Settings, Your data.
